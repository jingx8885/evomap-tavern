# Live2D sample assets

The default viewer model is official Live2D Cubism sample **Haru**, copied from
[Live2D/CubismWebSamples](https://github.com/Live2D/CubismWebSamples)
(`Samples/Resources/Haru`). `models/Mao` remains as the previous sample.

They remain under the [Live2D Free Material License Agreement](https://www.live2d.jp/en/terms/live2d-free-material-license-agreement/)
and the [Terms of Use for Live2D Cubism Sample Data](https://www.live2d.com/eula/live2d-sample-model-terms_en.html).

Cubism Core (`live2dcubismcore.min.js`) is loaded from Live2D's CDN at runtime
and is not redistributed in this tree.

`LICENSE.cubism.md` is the Cubism SDK sample license text from the upstream repo.

Haru motion `Sound` clips were removed from `Haru.model3.json` so sample Japanese
voice files do not play over the duplex session.
